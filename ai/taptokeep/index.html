How It Works

Three Steps. Zero Hassle.

1

Snap, Pick, or Share

See contact info on a web page, in a text, in an email, or in any image? Share it to TapToKeep from any app, or snap a picture.

2

AI Extracts

Google Gemini AI reads the content and pulls out contact details like name, phone, email, company, and social links.

3

Review & Save

Verify the extracted info, pick your address book, and save with one tap.

Features

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

🤖

AI-Powered Extraction

Google Gemini reads web pages, text messages, emails, images, and business cards. Names, titles, phone numbers, emails, websites, social profiles. All captured.

📥

Share From Any App

TapToKeep appears in your phone's share sheet. Share an image, web link, or text from Gmail, Safari, Photos, Messages, or anywhere. We'll read it.

📞

Smart Phone Formatting

Phone numbers are automatically formatted to international E.164 standard. Extensions are preserved with auto-dial support.

📕

Choose Your Address Book

Save to any account configured on your device. Google Contacts, Exchange, Samsung, or local phone contacts. You pick where each contact goes.

📷

Camera & Gallery

Snap a picture of anything with contact info, or pick an existing screenshot from your photo gallery.

🔗

Social Media Links

LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub, and other social profiles found in signatures are captured and saved to your contact.

🔒

Privacy You Can Read

Content you share or pick is sent over HTTPS to our secure service, which forwards it to Google Gemini AI on Google's Vertex AI platform for contact extraction. Uploaded images are deleted from our service within seconds of processing. We do not sell, share, or retain your content after the scan completes. The extracted contact details come back to your device for you to review and save.

💳

Share as vCard

Export any extracted contact as a standard vCard file. Share it via email, messaging, or AirDrop.

⚡

Free to Use

Free daily scans, no signup cost. For heavier use, an optional subscription is available in the app.
