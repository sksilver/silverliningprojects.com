Account & Data

Delete Your Data

Last updated: April 20, 2026

TapToKeep stores your session, profile, and daily scan counter on your device. Content you scan is processed by our secure service and deleted within seconds. We keep your Firebase Auth record (name, email, UID) on our backend so we can authenticate you across devices; you can delete that at any time with the steps below. Contacts you save go to your device's address book, not to us.

Option 1: Delete Account and All Data

This removes everything the app has stored on your device.

  1. Open the TapToKeep app
  2. Tap the trash icon in the top-right corner of the home screen
  3. Confirm by tapping "Delete"

What gets deleted:

  • Your Firebase sign-in session is cleared on this device
  • Your Firebase Auth record on our backend is deleted
  • Your scan counter, profile, and all locally stored preferences are wiped
  • Any server-side consent records tied to your account are deleted
  • TapToKeep never held broader access to your Google or Apple account; sign-in is identity only, so no third-party API tokens are used

What is NOT affected:

  • Your sign-in account itself (Google or Apple, not changed or deleted)
  • Contacts you already saved to your phone's address book
  • Your app store subscription (manage this in your device's app store settings)

Option 2: Sign Out Only

If you just want to disconnect your sign-in account (Google or Apple) without deleting all data:

  1. Open the TapToKeep app
  2. Tap the sign-out icon in the top-right corner of the home screen
  3. Confirm by tapping "Sign Out"

This ends your Firebase Auth session and returns you to the login screen. TapToKeep never holds scoped access to your Google or Apple account; your account itself is unaffected.

Option 3: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling TapToKeep from your device automatically removes all locally stored data, including your sign-in session, preferences, and scan counter. No data remains on your device after uninstalling.

Option 4: Email-Based Deletion Request

If you cannot access the in-app button (for example, you uninstalled the app), email support-ttk@silverliningprojects.com from the email address you signed in with. Include "Delete my account" in the subject. We will verify your email, delete your Firebase Auth record and any server-side data tied to your account, and confirm by reply within 30 days.

Data Stored by Google

When you use TapToKeep, content you share is processed by Google Gemini AI on Google's Vertex AI platform, under the Google Cloud Service Specific Terms. Google does not use your content to train its public AI models and deletes it after processing beyond operational logs. Your sign-in (if Google) is handled by Firebase Auth under Google's Privacy Policy. To manage data Google has stored on its consumer-account side, visit your Google Account Data & Privacy settings.
