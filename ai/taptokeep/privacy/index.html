Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

SilverLiningProjects ("we", "our", "us") built the TapToKeep app ("the App") as a free application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use the App.

Summary

TapToKeep is designed so SilverLiningProjects does not receive or store your data. The app has no SilverLiningProjects servers, databases, analytics, or trackers. When you choose content to convert — whether you pick an image, take a photo, or share an image, web link, or text to the app from another app — the content goes directly from your device to Google's Gemini AI using your Google account, and the extracted contact details are shown on your device so you can review and save them.

How the App Works

Google Sign-In: You sign in with your Google account using Google Sign-In. TapToKeep does not see your Google password. The app stores a Google access token on your device only to keep you signed in.

Image processing (Gemini AI): When you select a photo or take a picture to convert, the app sends only the image(s) you selected to Google's Gemini AI over HTTPS using your Google account's authorization. SilverLiningProjects is not in the network path and does not receive the images. You may select multiple images of the same contact and the app will merge them into a single contact record. Google's handling and retention of submitted content is governed by the Gemini API Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Share extension (images, web links, text): TapToKeep registers as a destination in your device's system share sheet. When you tap Share in any other app (Gmail, Safari, Photos, Messages, and so on) and pick TapToKeep, the content you shared — an image, a web link, or selected text — is handed to TapToKeep by the operating system. The app then processes that single piece of content using the same Gemini flow described above. Nothing else on your device is accessed or sent. If you share a web link, your device fetches the page and sends only the resulting text to Gemini for contact extraction. If you share text, the text is sent as-is. In all cases, SilverLiningProjects is not in the network path.

Photo library (optional delete after save): After you save a contact that was extracted from one or more photos you picked from your device's Photos library (including screenshots), TapToKeep may ask if you want to delete those specific source photo(s) from your Photos library. This is entirely optional. If you choose to delete, TapToKeep uses your operating system's targeted-delete API — on Android this is MediaStore.createDeleteRequest, on iOS this is PhotoKit.PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets. In both cases your operating system shows its own native confirmation dialog before any photo is removed. The app never requests or holds broad read access to your photo library (specifically, the app does not declare the Android READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission). The only photos the app can target are the specific ones you just processed in the same session. Photos taken with the in-app camera, photos shared into the app from other apps, and shared web links or text never trigger the delete prompt because they are not items in your Photos library.

Contact creation: The extracted contact data is displayed in the app for you to edit. If you choose to save it, the app writes the contact to your device's contacts provider (and any account you choose, such as Google Contacts). SilverLiningProjects does not receive the contact data.

Google User Data

TapToKeep requests the following Google OAuth scopes and accesses the following Google user data:

Data Accessed

  • Google Sign-In profile: Your name, email address, and profile photo are received from Google when you sign in. These are used only to display your identity within the app.
  • Gemini AI (generative-language.retriever scope): The app sends user-selected or user-shared content — images, fetched web page text, or shared text — to Google's Gemini API using your Google account's OAuth token. The API returns extracted contact text (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses). No other Google data is accessed.
  • Contacts (contacts scope): The app writes new contacts to your device's address book. It does not read, browse, or delete existing contacts.
  • Device Photos library (optional, user-initiated only): If you choose to delete a source photo after saving a contact, the app uses your operating system's targeted-delete API (MediaStore.createDeleteRequest on Android, PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets on iOS) to delete only the specific photo(s) you just processed. The app does NOT request broad access to your photo library — specifically, the app does not declare the Android READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission and uses iOS PhotoKit only with the user's explicit per-deletion consent. The app does not read, browse, list, or modify any other photo in your library. The operating system shows its own native confirmation dialog before any deletion occurs.
  • Shared content (system share sheet): When you send an image, web link, or text to TapToKeep from another app via the system share sheet, the operating system hands that single piece of content to the app. The app does not have access to any other content in the source app or on your device.

Data Usage

  • Your Google profile (name, email, photo) is used solely to display your identity in the app and to maintain your sign-in session.
  • Images, fetched web page text, and shared text are sent to Google Gemini exclusively for the purpose of extracting contact information. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • The contacts scope is used only to save extracted contacts to your device's address book.
  • The Photos library delete capability is used only, and only when you confirm, to remove the specific source photo(s) you just used for a successful contact save.

Data Sharing

  • SilverLiningProjects does not share any Google user data with any third party.
  • User-selected and user-shared content (images, fetched web page text, shared text) is transmitted directly from the user's device to Google's Gemini API. SilverLiningProjects is not in the transmission path and does not receive, intercept, or forward this data.
  • When you share a web link to the app, your device fetches the public page over HTTPS using the device's own network stack. That fetch is a normal browser-style request to the destination site. SilverLiningProjects is not involved and has no servers participating in the fetch.
  • No Google user data is shared for advertising, analytics, marketing, or any purpose other than the core app functionality described above.

Data Storage and Protection

  • SilverLiningProjects does not operate any servers or databases. No Google user data is stored on any infrastructure controlled by us.
  • Your Google OAuth access token and profile information are stored locally on your device in encrypted secure storage (Expo SecureStore).
  • A local scan counter (number of scans used today) is stored on your device in secure storage. It contains no personal information.
  • All communication between the app and Google services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • SilverLiningProjects retains no Google user data because we have no servers.
  • Locally stored data (OAuth token, profile, scan counter) is retained on your device until you sign out, delete your account data, or uninstall the app.
  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time by tapping the "Delete Account & Data" button in the app, or by uninstalling. For step-by-step instructions, visit our Delete Your Data page.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at your Google account permissions page.
  • Google's own retention of data submitted to the Gemini API is governed by Google's Gemini API Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

What We Collect

SilverLiningProjects does not collect, store, or receive personal data from TapToKeep. We do not run servers for this app, and we do not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

What Is Shared with Third Parties

To provide the app's core functionality, TapToKeep communicates with:

  • Google Sign-In (authentication)
  • Google Gemini AI (processing user-selected and user-shared content: images, fetched web page text, and shared text)
  • Public web pages you choose to share (when you share a web link to the app, your device fetches the page over HTTPS directly from the destination site; the resulting page text is then sent to Gemini)

These communications occur between your device and the listed third parties. SilverLiningProjects does not receive the content, but those parties may process and retain it as described in their own policies.

Data Storage

All data used by the app (your sign-in session, scan counter, and preferences) is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms. No data is stored in any cloud service, database, or server operated by SilverLiningProjects.

Data Deletion

Because SilverLiningProjects does not store your data, there is nothing we can delete on your behalf. You can remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or using the in-app "Delete Account & Data" option (which clears locally stored session and state). You can revoke Google access at your Google account permissions page. For step-by-step instructions, visit our Delete Your Data page.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, this concern does not apply in practice.

Changes to This Policy
