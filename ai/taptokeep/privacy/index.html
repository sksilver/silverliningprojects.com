Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Silver Lining Projects LLC ("we", "our", "us") built the TapToKeep app ("the App") as a free application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use the App.

Summary

TapToKeep extracts contact details from web pages, text, emails, or images you share with the app. Your shared content is sent to our secure service, which forwards it to Google Gemini AI for processing. The extracted contact data is returned to your device so you can review and save it. We do not store your shared content after processing completes.

Notice at Collection (California residents)

We collect these categories of personal information, as defined under Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140:

  • Identifiers (§ 1798.140(v)(1)(A)): name, email address, Firebase UID. Source: Google, Apple, or Facebook at sign in. Purpose: account authentication. Retention: until you delete your account or are inactive for 24 months.
  • Customer records information (§ 1798.140(v)(1)(B)): email address. Purpose: account authentication. Retention: same.
  • Commercial information (§ 1798.140(v)(1)(D)): subscription status, daily scan count. Purpose: billing, rate limits. Retention: device local; reset daily.
  • Internet or other electronic network activity information (§ 1798.140(v)(1)(F)): Cloud Function request logs (IP address, timestamp, user agent) retained up to 30 days for abuse prevention, then deleted.
  • Audio, electronic, visual, or similar information (§ 1798.140(v)(1)(I)): images you choose to scan. Retention: held only during processing (seconds), then auto deleted.
  • Content you submit for extraction: shared text, fetched web page text, email content. Retention: held only during processing, then auto deleted.

We do not collect: biometric information, geolocation data, professional or employment information, education information, or inferences used to create consumer profiles.

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months.

We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined in Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140(ae).

How the App Works

Sign in: You sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook. We use Firebase Auth, which confirms your identity and gives the app a short lived session token. TapToKeep never sees your Google, Apple, or Facebook password. The session token is stored on your device only to keep you signed in.

Share extension (web pages, text, emails, images): TapToKeep registers as a destination in your device's system share sheet. When you tap Share in any other app (Safari, Gmail, Photos, Messages, and so on) and pick TapToKeep, the content you shared is handed to the app by the operating system. The app sends that single piece of content to our secure service, which forwards it to Google Gemini AI for contact extraction. Nothing else on your device is accessed or sent.

Shared web links: When you share a web link, our secure service fetches the public page over HTTPS and sends only the resulting page text to Google Gemini AI. Your device does not fetch the page directly. This keeps page fetch safety protections (including checks that block requests to internal or private addresses) on a server we control instead of a device an attacker might compromise.

Image processing: When you pick a photo, take a picture, or share an image, the app uploads the image over HTTPS to our secure service. The service forwards the image to Google Gemini AI, receives the extracted contact data, and then deletes the uploaded image. The image is held only as long as processing takes, typically a few seconds.

Photo library (optional delete after save): After you save a contact that was extracted from one or more photos you picked from your device's Photos library (including screenshots), TapToKeep may ask if you want to delete those specific source photo(s) from your Photos library. This is entirely optional. If you choose to delete, TapToKeep uses your operating system's targeted delete API. On Android this is MediaStore.createDeleteRequest, on iOS this is PhotoKit.PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets. In both cases your operating system shows its own native confirmation dialog before any photo is removed. The app never requests or holds broad read access to your photo library (specifically, the app does not declare the Android READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission). The only photos the app can target are the specific ones you just processed in the same session. Photos taken with the in app camera, photos shared into the app from other apps, and shared web links or text never trigger the delete prompt because they are not items in your Photos library.

Contact creation: The extracted contact data is displayed in the app for you to edit. If you choose to save it, the app writes the contact to your device's contacts provider (and any account you choose, such as Google Contacts or iCloud). The contact data is written by your device and is not sent back to our service.

Data We Access and Process

Data Accessed

  • Sign in profile: Your name and email address are received from Google, Apple, or Facebook when you sign in. These are used only to maintain your signed in session and are not shown to other users.
  • Shared content (web pages, text, emails, images): When you pick, capture, or share content to the app, only that single item is sent to our secure service for processing by Google Gemini AI. The API returns extracted contact text (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses). No other content on your device is accessed.
  • Contacts (write only): The app writes new contacts to your device's address book when you confirm. It does not read, browse, or delete existing contacts.
  • Device Photos library (optional, user initiated only): If you choose to delete a source photo after saving a contact, the app uses your operating system's targeted delete API (MediaStore.createDeleteRequest on Android, PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets on iOS) to delete only the specific photo(s) you just processed. The app does NOT request broad access to your photo library.

Data Usage

  • Your sign in profile (name, email) is used solely to maintain your signed in session.
  • Shared content is forwarded to Google Gemini AI exclusively for the purpose of extracting contact information.
  • The write to contacts capability is used only to save extracted contacts to your device's address book when you confirm.
  • The Photos library delete capability is used only, and only when you confirm, to remove the specific source photo(s) you just used for a successful contact save.

Data Sharing

  • Silver Lining Projects LLC does not sell or share your data with any third party for advertising, analytics, marketing, or any purpose other than the core app functionality described above.
  • User shared content (web pages, text, emails, images) passes through our secure service to reach Google Gemini AI for processing. Our service does not store the content after processing completes.
  • When you share a web link, our service fetches the public page over HTTPS on your behalf. That fetch is a normal server side request to the destination site.

Data Storage and Protection

  • Silver Lining Projects LLC operates a secure service hosted on Google Cloud to process your shared content. No user content is stored on our service after processing completes.
  • Uploaded images are held in temporary storage only for the duration of a scan (typically a few seconds), and are deleted automatically when processing completes.
  • Your Firebase Auth session token and profile information are stored locally on your device in encrypted secure storage (Expo SecureStore).
  • A local scan counter (number of scans used today) is stored on your device in secure storage. It contains no personal information.
  • All communication between the app and our service, and between our service and Google Gemini AI, uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Silver Lining Projects LLC does not retain your shared content after processing completes.
  • Locally stored data (session token, profile, scan counter) is retained on your device until you sign out, delete your account data, or uninstall the app.
  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time by tapping the "Delete Account & Data" button in the app, or by uninstalling. For step by step instructions, visit our Delete Your Data page.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at your Google account permissions page.
  • Google's retention of data submitted to Google Cloud is governed by the Google Cloud Service Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

What We Collect

Silver Lining Projects LLC does not collect, store, or retain personal data from TapToKeep beyond the duration of a single scan request. We do not include analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

AI Disclosure

TapToKeep uses a generative AI system (Google Gemini on Google's Vertex AI platform, operated by Google LLC) to extract contact information from content you submit. You are interacting with an AI system (EU AI Act Art. 50). The AI does not make any decision with legal or similarly significant effect; it proposes text for your review, and every saved contact requires your explicit confirmation.

Content sent to Google through Vertex AI is processed under Google Cloud's Vertex AI Service Specific Terms, which prohibit Google from using your content to train, improve, or fine-tune Google's public AI models. Google retains the content only for a short abuse detection logging window and then deletes it.
