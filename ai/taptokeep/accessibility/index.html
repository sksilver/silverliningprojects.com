Accessibility Statement

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Silver Lining Projects LLC is committed to making TapToKeep accessible to people with a wide range of abilities. We design, build, and maintain the app and this website with accessibility as a continuing priority, not an afterthought.

We target conformance to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2, Level AA (WCAG 2.2 AA), in good faith. Where an area does not yet meet that target, we document the gap and work to resolve it. We do not claim full conformance; we claim substantial conformance under an active program of improvement.

Areas still in progress as of today include self-hosted fonts (we are migrating away from the Google Fonts CDN to remove an IP-disclosure step before user consent), a formal accessibility testing log across each shipping build, and an automated third-party audit scheduled before public launch. You can review our current compliance documents in our repository under the docs/compliance/ folder.

If you run into an accessibility barrier using TapToKeep or this website, please email support-ttk@silverliningprojects.com with a short description of the issue and the device or assistive technology you were using. We acknowledge every report within five business days and share a remediation plan or interim workaround in that window.

The email inbox above is staffed by Silver Lining Projects LLC directly. If you prefer a different contact path (mailing address or a telephone follow-up), include that preference in your message and we will accommodate.

This statement applies to the TapToKeep mobile app on iOS and Android, and to the TapToKeep pages on this website: the landing page at /ai/taptokeep/, the privacy policy, the terms of use, the delete-your-data page, and this accessibility statement. We will update this statement as our conformance posture changes.
