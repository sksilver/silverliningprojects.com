1. Description of Service

TapToKeep uses Google Gemini AI to extract contact information from web pages, text, emails, or images you share with the App. The App registers with your operating system's share sheet, so you can send a web link, text, an email, or an image to TapToKeep from any other app on your device. You may also pick an image from your photo library or take one with the camera. You may select multiple images of the same contact and the App will merge them into a single contact record. The App never automatically scans, browses, or processes your photos or other content. It only uses the specific items you select or explicitly share to it each time. Extracted contacts are saved to your device's address book. After saving a contact that was created from one or more photos you picked from your device's Photos library, the App may ask if you want to delete those specific source photo(s); this is optional, initiated by you, and confirmed by your operating system before any deletion happens.

3. AI Processing and Data

When you select or share content to convert, the App sends that content to our secure service over HTTPS. Our service forwards the content to Google Gemini AI on Google's Vertex AI platform (operated by Google LLC) and returns the extracted contact data to your device. For shared web links, our service fetches the public page over HTTPS on your behalf and sends only the resulting page text to Google Gemini AI. For shared text, the text is sent as is. For images, the image is uploaded to temporary storage on our service, processed, and deleted when processing completes. In all cases, no other content on your device is sent or accessed. Silver Lining Projects LLC does not retain your shared content after processing completes. Google's handling of data submitted to Google Cloud is governed by the Google Cloud Service Specific Terms (Vertex AI section) and the Cloud Data Processing Addendum and Google's Privacy Policy. See our Privacy Policy for details on our data handling practices.
