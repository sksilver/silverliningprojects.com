1. Description of Service

TapToKeep is a mobile application that uses Google Gemini AI to extract contact information from email signatures, business cards, websites, text messages, or any content containing contact details. The App requests your permission to access your camera and photo gallery so you can take a photo or choose one or more screenshots, and you may also use your operating system's share sheet to send an image, a web link, or a text selection to the App from any other app on your device. You may select multiple images of the same contact and the App will merge them into a single contact record. The App never automatically scans, browses, or processes your photos or other content. It only uses the specific items you select or explicitly share to it each time. Extracted contacts are saved to your device's address book. After saving a contact that was created from one or more photos you picked from your device's Photos library, the App may ask if you want to delete those specific source photo(s); this is optional, initiated by you, and confirmed by your operating system before any deletion happens.

3. AI Processing and Data

When you select or share content to convert, that content is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API using your own Google account credentials. For images, the selected image file is sent. For shared web links, your device fetches the public page over HTTPS from the destination site and sends only the resulting page text to Gemini. For shared text, the text is sent as-is. In all cases, no other content on your device is sent or accessed. Silver Lining Projects LLC does not receive, store, or have access to your content or extracted contact data. Google may retain submitted content per their terms of service. Google's handling of data submitted to the Gemini API is governed by the Gemini API Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Optional Photo Deletion

After you save a contact that was extracted from one or more photos you picked from your device's Photos library (including screenshots), the App may offer to delete those specific source photo(s) from your Photos library. This is entirely optional and can only happen if you explicitly confirm both the in-app prompt and the operating system's native deletion dialog. The App uses your operating system's targeted-delete API (MediaStore.createDeleteRequest on Android, PHAssetChangeRequest.deleteAssets on iOS) which is designed specifically to allow apps to request deletion of individual user-identified media items without holding broad photo library permissions. The App never requests or holds broad read access to your photo library. Specifically, the App does not declare the Android READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission. The only photos the App can target are the specific ones you just processed in the same session. Photos taken with the in-app camera, photos that were shared into the App from another app via the system share sheet, and shared web links or text never trigger the delete prompt because they are not items in your Photos library. Silver Lining Projects LLC is not liable for any photo that you choose to delete through this optional flow.
