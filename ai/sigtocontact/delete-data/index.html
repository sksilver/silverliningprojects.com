Account & Data

Delete Your Data

Last updated: April 6, 2026

SnapToContact stores all data locally on your device. We have no servers, no databases, and no copies of your data. Deleting your data is instant and permanent.

Option 1: Delete Account and All Data

This removes everything the app has stored on your device.

  1. Open the SnapToContact app
  2. Tap the trash icon in the top-right corner of the home screen
  3. Confirm by tapping "Delete"

What gets deleted:

  • Your Google Sign-In session
  • Your scan counter and usage history
  • All locally stored preferences and settings
  • Your Google access token is revoked (the app can no longer access your account)

What is NOT affected:

  • Your Google account itself (it is not changed or deleted)
  • Contacts you already saved to your phone's address book
  • Your app store subscription (manage this in your device's app store settings)

Option 2: Sign Out Only

If you just want to disconnect your Google account without deleting all data:

  1. Open the SnapToContact app
  2. Tap the sign-out icon in the top-right corner of the home screen
  3. Confirm by tapping "Sign Out"

This revokes the app's access to your Google account and returns you to the login screen.

Option 3: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling SnapToContact from your device automatically removes all locally stored data, including your sign-in session, preferences, and scan counter. No data remains on your device after uninstalling.

Data Stored by Google

When you use SnapToContact, images you select are sent to Google's Gemini AI using your own Google account. Google may retain this data per their terms of service. To manage data Google has stored, visit your Google Account Data & Privacy settings.
