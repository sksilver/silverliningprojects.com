1. Description of Service

SigToContact is a mobile application that uses Google Gemini AI to extract contact information from email signatures, business cards, websites, text messages, or any image containing contact details. The App requests your permission to access your camera and photo gallery so you can take a photo or choose one or more screenshots. You may select multiple images of the same contact and the App will merge them into a single contact record. The App never automatically scans, browses, or processes your photos — it only uses the specific images you select each time. Extracted contacts are saved to your device's address book.
